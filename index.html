Will this pack work on your Bedrock server?

Drop in the file you downloaded. We tell you if it is made for Bedrock or Java, which folder it goes in, and the exact text to paste into your world file.

The Pack Checker needs JavaScript to run. Turn it on in your browser, or follow the manual steps in our install guide.

What it tells you

Bedrock or Java
Java packs, mods and plugins do not run on Bedrock servers. You find out before you upload anything.
Pack type
Behavior packs go in behavior_packs, resource (texture) packs go in resource_packs. Addon bundles with both are split up for you.
Upload folder
Which folder inside the download to take, or what to name a new one when the pack has none.
World file entry
The pack ID and version for world_behavior_packs.json or world_resource_packs.json, ready to copy.
Missing pieces
A warning when a pack needs another pack that is not in your file.

Your file stays on your computer

The check runs inside your browser. Your file is never uploaded to MintServers or anywhere else.

Questions

Which files can I check?

Files ending in .mcpack, .mcaddon or .zip. You can also drop in a .jar to confirm it is a Java mod or plugin. If your download is a .rar or .7z, extract it on your computer first and check the file inside.

It says my pack is for Java. What now?

Look for the Bedrock version of the same pack. On MCPEDL it is usually a .mcpack or .mcaddon file. You can also browse ready-made packs in the Bedrock Addons tab of your MintServers panel.

What do I do after the check?

Follow the steps it shows you. For the full walkthrough with screenshots, read the install guide.

It could not read my file, or the result looks wrong.

Download the pack again in case the first download was cut off. If it still fails, open a support ticket from your MintServers account and attach the file. Our team will take a look.
